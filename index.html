How it plays

Easy to learn, satisfying to master

PawDuo takes the classic binary puzzle and fills it with friendly animals. Each board has exactly one solution, and you can always reach it by logic alone. A short tutorial teaches the rules in a minute; the levels then grow from gentle warm-ups to real brain-teasers.

No three in a row

Never more than two of the same animal side by side, across or down.

Keep it balanced

Every row and every column holds the same number of each animal.

No twins

No two rows are the same, and no two columns are the same.

Why PawDuo

More than just another grid

Pure logic, zero guessing

1,000 levels, each with exactly one solution you can reach step by step. If you're stuck, there's always a reason to find.

Daily League

Three fresh puzzles every day. Solve them fast, rise on the daily ranking and climb from Bronze to Grandmaster.

Hints that teach

A hint doesn't just fill a cell. It tells you why that animal belongs there, so every hint makes you a better solver.

Collect animal duos

Earn Forest Tokens and unlock new pairs, like Pig & Snake or Giraffe & Zebra, plus board skins to play your way.

Daily missions

Small goals every day, from solving a level without a reset to finishing ten levels, each with a token reward.

Make it yours

Choose an animal avatar and show off frames earned in the league. Your rank, your style.
