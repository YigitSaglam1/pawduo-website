Become a Tester

Play PawDuo before it launches. Try new builds first, tell us what feels great and what doesn't, and help shape the game.

Join the test

For Android phones. Free to join, and you can leave the test at any time.

How it works

Three steps to your first test build

1

Open the invite

On your Android phone, tap Join the test and enter the email address you want to use for testing.

2

Accept the email

You'll get an invitation email from Firebase App Distribution. Open it on your phone and accept.

3

Install and play

Follow the on-screen steps to install PawDuo. Android may ask you to allow installing apps from this source. When a new build is ready, you'll get an email.

What you get

New levels, features and animal duos before anyone else, and a direct line to the people making the game.

What we ask

Play when you like and tell us what you think: bugs, confusing moments, levels that felt too easy or too hard. Send it to support@pawduogame.com.

Test builds are early versions and may have bugs. Invitations and installs are handled by Google's Firebase App Distribution, which uses the email address you enter to send them. See our Privacy Policy for details.
