PawDuo

Two animals. Three rules. One perfect solution.

An animal-themed logic puzzle for mobile, looking for a publishing partner. Fill a 6×6 board with two animals so every row and column follows three simple rules. Every board has exactly one solution, reachable by logic alone.

Why it can work

A proven puzzle with a fresh, friendly skin

PawDuo takes the binary puzzle format (Binairo / Takuzu), which has a large and loyal audience, and gives it a warm, character-driven presentation, with retention and monetization systems built in from day one.

Proven core, fresh skin

Binary puzzles are easy to learn and highly replayable. Animal pairs instead of 0s and 1s make them approachable for casual players.

Content depth

1,000 curated levels with a wave-shaped difficulty curve, each verified to have one unique, logic-only solution.

Daily habit loop

A Daily League with three new puzzles every day and promotion and relegation, plus three daily missions with rewards.

Meta progression

Coins unlock new animal pairs and board skins; profile frames are earned through the league and level milestones.

Monetization ready

Rewarded ads for hints and interstitials between levels, integrated with Google AdMob and Google's UMP consent flow.

Measurable

GameAnalytics tracks progression, hint and ad events, so retention and funnel data are available from the first test.

Inside the game

Gameplay, retention and monetization

Core gameplay

  • Three rules: no more than two of the same animal side by side; every line holds the same number of each animal; no two rows or columns are the same.
  • Pure logic: puzzles are generated and checked by a solver, then sorted by the techniques they require.
  • Smooth curve: the first 15 levels are the easiest in the pool; after that, difficulty rises in waves, each ending with a peak level.
  • Guided tutorial: short, skippable and replayable from Settings.
  • Hints that teach: a hint explains why an animal belongs in a cell.
  • Never lose progress: an unfinished board resumes where it was left.

Retention systems

  • Daily League: three ranked puzzles a day, scored by solving time. Groups of 20; the top 5 are promoted, the bottom 5 relegated; harder puzzles at higher tiers.
  • Daily missions: three random missions a day from a varied pool, each with a coin reward.
  • Collection: seven animal pairs and several board skins, unlocked with coins earned by playing.
  • Profile: 30 animal avatars, a player name, and frames earned through league tiers and level milestones.

Monetization

  • Rewarded ads: the first hint in each level is free; further hints are unlocked by watching a rewarded video, with no limit.
  • Interstitials between normal levels. The Daily League has none, to keep ranked play fair and focused.
  • Google AdMob with the UMP consent flow (GDPR ready).
  • Room to grow: the coin economy and cosmetics are ready for optional in-app purchases, such as coin packs or an ad-free option.

What makes it different

More than just another grid

  1. Warm theme on a cold genre

    Most binary puzzle apps are plain grids of numbers or colors. PawDuo uses cute animal pairs with distinct shapes as well as colors, so boards are easy to read.

  2. Competitive daily loop

    A daily league with promotion and relegation is rare in this genre and gives players a reason to come back every day.

  3. Hints that teach

    Players get better as they play, which keeps them engaged with harder levels instead of churning.

  4. Quality-checked content

    Every one of the 1,000 levels is solver-verified to have one unique, logic-only solution.

  5. Short sessions

    6×6 boards fit a few-minute break, which suits ad pacing and daily play.

  6. No account needed

    Players start instantly, with no sign-up friction.

  7. Data from day one

    Analytics and consent-compliant ads are already in place for soft-launch testing.
