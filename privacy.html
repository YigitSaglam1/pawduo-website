Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026

This policy explains what information the PawDuo mobile game ("the app") uses, why, and the choices you have. PawDuo is developed by an independent developer ("we", "us").

Information we do not collect

PawDuo has no accounts and no sign-in. We do not run our own servers for the game, and we do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or photos, except an email address you choose to give us as described under "Feedback emails" and "Beta testing" below.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (completed levels, Forest Tokens, unlocked animals, frames and board skins, Daily League results, settings and the player name you choose) is saved only on your device. It is not sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob: an optional video you can watch for an extra hint, and occasional full-screen ads between levels. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA, where permitted),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version),
  • interactions with ads (for example, views and taps) and diagnostic data.

Google uses this information as described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • Consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland): the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Settings > Privacy options in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Android: Settings > Google > Ads / Privacy > Ads; iOS: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking).
  • Without internet: the game can be played offline; no ads are loaded then.

Feedback emails

If you contact us by email (for example with the Feedback button in the app), we receive your email address and whatever you write, and use it only to reply to you. You can ask us to delete that correspondence at any time.

Beta testing (Firebase App Distribution)

Joining the PawDuo test program is optional and is for Android devices only. Test builds are delivered through Google's Firebase App Distribution. When you join from our Become a Tester page, the following is processed by Google and visible to us in Firebase:

  • the email address you enter, used to send you invitations and notices about new test builds,
  • whether you accepted the invitation and which test builds you downloaded,
  • basic device information Firebase reports for testers (such as device model and Android version).

We use this only to run the test and to contact you about it. We do not use it for advertising and do not share it with anyone else. You can leave the test at any time; email us and we will remove you and your data from the test. Google processes this data under the Firebase privacy information and the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

PawDuo is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device until you delete the app or its data. Emails are kept only as long as needed to answer them. Tester data is kept while you are in the test program and removed when you leave it or the test ends. Data processed by Google is retained under Google's own policies.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold game data on our side, most requests concern Google's advertising data; you can manage these through the choices above or contact us for help.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the app changes (for example if online features are added). The date at the top shows the latest version.
